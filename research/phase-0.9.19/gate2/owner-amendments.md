research question, A/B same-generation requirement and deterministic scoring remain.

## Ground Truth authority

Owner delegated scenario and Ground Truth review to this assistant and directed that
the reviewed content be authoritative. This is **owner-delegated-model-review**, not
independent human review. The same assistant may author and review; inventing a second
reviewer name or claiming human authenticity is prohibited.

The reviewed/frozen oracle is the operational scoring authority for this development
study. A deterministic evaluator alone grades actual outputs. This delegation does not
authorize post-output oracle edits, retrospective prompt tuning, a second model judge,
or scoring the assistant's own subjective impressions of experimental results.

Provenance and bias must be reported: model-authored/reviewed labels may share systematic
errors with the tested model and do not establish independent human validity. Future
confirmatory or external-validity claims require a separately declared design and approval;
this amendment does not silently generalize development scores to real user work.
