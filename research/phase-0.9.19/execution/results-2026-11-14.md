## Why the experiment stopped

The evidence sink consumed 97.2% of t2 client time and 97.3% of t3 client time.
The journal had 3267 events / 1,898,350 bytes after three attempts. A read-only full
replay took 227-231 ms in three measurements after the run had halted.

The append path in run-ledger.mjs runs exclusive(replay) for EVERY capture event;
replay revalidates all previous events/chunk bytes. t1/t2/t3 produced 576/1617/1068
capture events respectively. Repeated full-history work grows with accumulated
events and dominates synchronous stream handling. This is a Harness performance
defect exposed by real chunk frequency, not evidence that model inference needed
180 seconds. The earlier single-block synthetic Responses did not adequately stress
this path, despite passing protocol/integrity tests.

Classification: experimental-condition / synchronous evidence-sink bottleneck.
The missing-header failure is separately classified as transport-protocol adaptation.
State exact-match failures are real frozen-score results but have limited support
and evaluator-reference sensitivity; no general model-capability verdict follows.
