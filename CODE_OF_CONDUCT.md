# ACMI Code of Conduct

We expect everyone to help make ACMI welcoming and accessible. Respect others'
identities, backgrounds, and experience. Critique ideas and code constructively,
consider the impact of your words, and accept responsibility when you cause harm.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory or demeaning remarks, unwanted sexual
attention, impersonation intended to harm, and sharing someone's private
information without permission are not acceptable.

This policy applies in ACMI community spaces and when someone officially
represents ACMI. Maintainers may remove harmful material, issue a warning,
restrict participation, or ban a participant, depending on severity and repeated
behavior. Maintainers should explain decisions when appropriate and limit the
disclosure of incident details.

If you have a private way to reach a project maintainer, use it to report an
incident. Otherwise use the project's [support route](SUPPORT.md) to request
private follow-up without posting the report or identifying anyone involved.
For violations of GitHub's own policies, you can also use
[GitHub's abuse reporting tools](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam).
